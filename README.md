I’m currently studying at the **University of Peradeniya**, passionate about building systems that connect **software**, **data**, and **hardware**. 

- 🔭 I’m actively looking to collaborate on projects involving **Python**, **FastAPI**, **Jinja**, **PyTorch**, and **IoT**.
- 💡 I'm particularly interested in where **embedded systems** and **data processing** meet.
- 🤝 I enjoy working on:
